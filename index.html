Project Runway Corp

I founded Project Runway Corp in New York in August 2022 and operated it through December 2024 as a ticket resale business.

The business purchased tickets for concerts, sports and other live events and resold them through secondary marketplaces. I handled ticket sourcing, pricing, listings, order fulfillment, payout reconciliation and the automation used to keep inventory and records organized.

What the business did

I looked for ticket inventory where the expected resale price justified the cost and risk. After purchasing inventory, I listed it through resale and consignment marketplaces, adjusted pricing as events approached, fulfilled sales and reconciled the resulting payouts.

I also built internal automation for processing order confirmations and PDF tickets, tagging inventory and matching marketplace payouts to sales records. The business ceased operating at the end of 2024.
