03 / Definitions

No fuzzy math.

Payouts

Amounts shown as paid in the Lysted remittance portal. These are the figures used for the 2022–2024 operating ledger above.

1099-K gross amount

Gross reportable payments stated by the payment settlement entity. It is not a statement of profit, margin, cash on hand or company valuation.

Line items

Rows within authenticated remittance records. A line item should not be assumed to equal one order, one ticket or one customer.

Source scope: authenticated Lysted remittance records reviewed for August 2022–December 2024 and the issuer-provided 2023 Form 1099-K. Totals are historical payment volume, not net income.
